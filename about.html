About WHO

A structured identity reference platform that answers: “Who is this person, and where can I find reliable information about them?”

What WHO is

WHO helps people, search engines, AI assistants, research systems and autonomous agents discover reliable, structured information about real people — their identities, professional backgrounds, projects, contributions and official contact channels.

It is designed as both a readable editorial website and a machine-accessible source of entity information (HTML, structured data and optional JSON profiles).

What WHO is not

  • Not a social network
  • Not a job board
  • Not a conventional portfolio builder
  • Not a place for unverified claims or private data

Positioning

WHO sits at the intersection of personal knowledge management, professional identity discovery, open-source attribution, search-engine entity discovery and AI-assisted web research. Initial focus is developers, creators, open-source contributors and independent technology builders, with a foundation for broader categories later.
